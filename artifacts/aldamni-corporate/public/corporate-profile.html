Auto Service App for General Trading (أوتو سيرفس اب للتجارة العامة) is a regional trading company established in 2019, operating between Kuwait and Syria across petroleum supply, automotive products, fine foods and hydroponic agriculture.

Our sectors

Petroleum & energy

Petroleum supply and related logistics, including supply to the Syrian Ministry of Petroleum.

Auto service & lubricants

Auto Service App spare parts and Auto-Oil 5000, 10000 and 15000 grades: 5W-30, 10W-40 and 20W-50, in 1L and 4L bottles.

Fine food products

Shamco Wafrat El-Sham: olive oil, Spanish black and green sliced olives, feta and Akkawi/Syrian cheese with black seed and sesame, Kalamata and grilled olives.

Hydroponic agriculture

Greenhouse growing in Syria, with local lettuce, tomatoes and cucumbers.

Regional presence

Our business hubs in Al-Aqaila, Kuwait and Rif Dimashq, Syria support regional sourcing, supply and product development.
