## Platform

web

## Users

The site serves both institutional buyers and regional supply partners. Buyers assess the
company's sectors and reach the appropriate office; supply partners use it to understand the
company's operating scope and start a business enquiry.

## Product Purpose

The website introduces Auto Service App for General Trading, explains its operating sectors and
regional presence, and routes business enquiries to the Kuwait office, Syria office, or company
email.

## Positioning

The company combines petroleum supply, automotive parts and its own lubricants, food imports for
Kuwait cooperative societies, and hydroponic agriculture under a Kuwait-established trading
company operating across Kuwait and Syria.

## Operating Context

Visitors may be evaluating supply capability, product sourcing, regional operations, or a business
relationship. The site is bilingual in English and Arabic and serves visitors in both Kuwait and
Syria.
